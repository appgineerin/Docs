# Version Lock

Inside an application's Settings tab is a **Version Lock** toggle. When enabled, the Launcher will not auto-update the app — useful when you need to stay on a known-good version for compatibility with a project, a recording, or a broadcast.

![Screenshot: A close-up of the Version Lock toggle in the application Settings tab, in its enabled state, with descriptive helper text beneath it.](/img/launcher/version-lock.png)

Locking a version does not hide updates: newer versions still appear in the [Versions tab](./version-history.md) with their **Update** buttons. You can update manually at any time by clicking **Update** on the version you want. Locking simply prevents automatic updates from being applied without your explicit action.

Version lock does not affect [mandatory updates](./updates.md#mandatory-updates) — if the publisher flags a version as required, the mandatory-update warning still appears and the app cannot be launched until you apply it.
